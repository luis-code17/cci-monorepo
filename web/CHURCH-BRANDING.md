## Branding Principles Applied

### Tone & Voice
✅ **Warm & Welcoming**: All copy invites people to join the community  
✅ **Spiritual & Modern**: Balances faith with contemporary language  
✅ **Community-Focused**: Emphasizes service, growth, and belonging  
✅ **Spanish Language**: 100% Spanish UI/copy (all visible content)  

### Visual Direction Maintained
✅ Soft light backgrounds  
✅ Elegant serif headings (Cormorant Garamond)  
✅ Spacious, editorial layouts  
✅ Calm, spiritual atmosphere  
✅ DaisyUI themes (light, cupcake, dark)  

### Content Themes
✅ **Fe (Faith)**: Emphasized in mission statements  
✅ **Comunidad (Community)**: Highlighted throughout  
✅ **Propósito (Purpose)**: In messaging and calls to action  
✅ **Esperanza (Hope)**: Present in hero and taglines  
✅ **Crecimiento Espiritual (Spiritual Growth)**: Featured in services and content  
✅ **Familia (Family)**: Multi-generational inclusive language  

### What We Avoided
❌ Corporate startup feeling  
❌ Dark or aggressive design  
❌ Overly traditional aesthetics  
❌ Generic placeholder copy  
❌ Aggressive fundraising language
